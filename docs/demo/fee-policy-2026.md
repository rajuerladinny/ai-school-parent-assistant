# Sunrise Valley School - Fee Policy 2026 (Demo)


## Payment schedule

School fees are paid in three equal instalments, one per term. Each instalment is due by the 10th of the first month of the term: 10 January, 10 April and 10 September.

## Payment methods

Fees can be paid by bank transfer or by card at the school office. Cash payments are no longer accepted from 2026 onwards.

## Late payments

If an instalment is more than 14 days late, the school office will contact the family to agree a payment plan. Families experiencing financial difficulty are encouraged to speak to the office in confidence before the due date.

## Sibling discount

Families with more than one child enrolled at Sunrise Valley receive a 10% discount on fees for the second and each additional child.

## Refunds

If a student leaves the school, a full term's written notice is required. Fees for a term that has already started are not refunded.
