# Sunrise Valley School - Parent Handbook 2026 (Demo)


## School day

The school day runs Monday to Friday. Gates open at 8:00 AM, and morning registration starts at 8:30 AM. Lessons end at 3:30 PM. On Fridays, lessons end early at 1:30 PM so staff can attend training.

Students who arrive after 8:45 AM must sign in at the front office with a parent or guardian.

## Uniform

Students wear the navy blue school sweater, a white collared shirt, and grey trousers or a grey skirt. Plain black school shoes are required; trainers are only allowed on physical education days. The physical education kit is a white T-shirt with navy shorts. Every item of uniform should be labelled with the student's name.

## Lunch and snacks

The school canteen serves a hot lunch every day. A weekly lunch menu is posted on the noticeboard by the main entrance every Friday. Students may bring a packed lunch instead. Sunrise Valley is a nut-free school, so please do not send any food containing nuts.

## Absence and illness

If your child is ill, phone the school office before 9:00 AM on each day of absence. Children who have had vomiting or diarrhoea should stay at home until 48 hours after the last episode. For planned absences, such as medical appointments, send a note to the class teacher at least two days in advance.

## Transport

The school runs two morning bus routes, the North route and the River route. Buses leave the last stop at 8:05 AM and return students after school at 3:45 PM. Bus places are limited and are allocated at the start of each term by the school office.

## Library

The school library is open from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM on school days. Students may borrow up to three books at a time for two weeks. Lost or damaged books should be reported to the librarian.

## After-school clubs

After-school clubs run from 3:30 PM to 4:30 PM, Monday to Thursday. Current clubs are chess, football, art, coding and choir. Sign-up forms are sent home at the start of each term, and places are offered on a first-come, first-served basis.

## Parent-teacher meetings

Parent-teacher meetings take place twice a year, in October and in March. Appointment slots are booked through the class teacher. Parents can also request a meeting with a teacher at any time by contacting the school office.

## Contacting the school

The school office is open from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM on weekdays. For general questions, contact the office. For questions about a specific student, speak to the class teacher. Please do not share personal student details through the Parent Assistant chat.
